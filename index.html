Frequently Asked Questions

Common Clinical Inquiries

All major surgical procedures are performed exclusively in NABH-accredited modular operating rooms at Max Healthcare Hospitals (Max Super Speciality Hospital Vaishali, Max Multi Speciality Hospital Noida, and Max Patparganj) with full anaesthetic, ICU, and inpatient support.
Reconstructive surgery focuses on repairing physical defects caused by trauma, burns, cancer, birth conditions, or infections to restore normal bodily function and appearance. Aesthetic (cosmetic) surgery reshapes and refines normal features to enhance physical proportion and boost personal self-confidence. Dr. Johar's group provides the complete spectrum of both.
Treatment cost depends on the procedure type, surgical complexity, required anaesthesia, implant choices (if any), and room category at Max Healthcare. Following your comprehensive clinical evaluation with Dr. Johar, you receive a transparent, itemized estimate.
Reconstructive surgeries necessitated by trauma, cancer, burn deformities, cleft repair, functional breathing obstruction (septoplasty), and amputation prevention are generally eligible for cashless insurance coverage under standard policy terms at Max Healthcare. Purely elective cosmetic procedures are generally self-pay.
Outstation and global patients can schedule a secure virtual video consultation via our International Desk. You can submit previous reports and photographs for preliminary medical evaluation, duration planning, and travel scheduling.
