Complete Guide to Rhinoplasty Surgery

8 Jun 2026SurgicalBy Dr. Johar's Team

Rhinoplasty is one of the most requested — and most technically demanding — aesthetic procedures. Because the nose sits at the centre of the face, even small changes have a big effect on overall harmony.

What rhinoplasty can do

  • Reduce a dorsal hump and straighten the bridge
  • Refine a bulbous or drooping tip
  • Narrow wide nostrils
  • Correct asymmetry after injury
  • Improve breathing by straightening the septum (septorhinoplasty)

Open vs closed technique

In a closed rhinoplasty all incisions are inside the nostrils. In an open rhinoplasty a tiny incision across the columella allows the surgeon to see the framework directly — useful for tip work and revisions. The resulting scar is almost invisible once healed.

The day of surgery

Rhinoplasty is performed under general anaesthesia and takes two to three hours. Most patients go home the same day or after one night with a small external splint.

Recovery timeline

  • Week 1: splint on, bruising around the eyes settles
  • Week 2: presentable in public, back to work
  • Weeks 4–6: most swelling gone; exercise resumed
  • Months 6–12: the tip refines to its final shape

Choosing your surgeon

Because the nose must both look natural and function well, choose a plastic surgeon with a structural approach who will discuss realistic goals with you — and who can show you results on faces like yours.
