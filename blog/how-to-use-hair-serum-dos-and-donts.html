How to use hair serum: Dos and Don'ts

10 Jun 2022Hair CareBy Dr. Johar's Team

Is your hair starting to dry out and losing its bounce? Beautiful, long, smoothed hair is all the rage these days. Trying to figure out how to stay on trend? You don't have to ponder any longer; the competition with your hairbrush is over. Hair growth serum, which is multifunctional, is one go-to treatment for all your hair concerns. To boost hair renewal, Hair Growth Serum should be a must-have in your daily hair care routine. So, if you're considering the advantages of using hair serum, read on to learn more about the numerous advantages of hair serum and how to apply it correctly.

How does Hair Growth Serum Work?

Hair serums, also known as styling products, help to add extra shine and smoothness to your hair while also precisely setting up your hairdo. Hair Serum is a liquid solution designed with active components high in silicone content, which helps control frizz and protects your hair from environmental plunderers. Silicone component in hair serums is responsible for the strength and radiance of your hair. Hair serums function on the surface of your hair, as opposed to hair oils, which feed your scalp. Hair serums contain amino acids that penetrate deep into your hair, reducing breakage and increasing hair regrowth. Dimethicone and polysiloxane are two more typical components in hair care products that help protect your hair from heat damage. Hair serums, which provide a protective coating for the surface of your hair, can be used as a quick remedy or even as a conditioner replacement. Hair serums will also assist you in managing your hair like an expert.

Hair Growth Serum's Advantages

Controlling Frizz

Hair development serums can help you get out of your suffering and provide good nutrients to your frizzy hair. You may have flyaways or stray hair that lies against the rest of your hair, in addition to frizzy or dry hair that appears damaged and frail. To get out of this bind, hair growth serum offers advantages that are the ideal solution to all of these problems. Silicon in hair serum aids in the management of frizz and flyaways in your hair.

Provides Smoothness

Silicone works by coating the surface of your hair, resulting in an anti-frizz effect and the appearance of smooth and polished strands. This method aids in increasing the smoothness of coarse hair.

Adds shine

Aside from preventing frizz and flyaways, hair smoothness automatically results in a rich, gleaming texture. Furthermore, the types of silicone utilized in hair serums contribute to the overall shine. If you include a shine-boosting hair growth serum in your daily hair care routine, your hair will have a long-lasting shine.

How To Use Hair Serum?

Application Instructions:

The serum should be applied to moist, freshly washed hair. It adds a layer of protection to your hair this way. Begin at the ends of your hair and work your way up to the center regions. Applying serum to your scalp might cause your hair to appear oily and greasy. In fact, to avoid product buildup, we recommend completely ignoring the roots. Remember that your ends require the greatest shine, so apply it there!

How Much Should You Use:

Serums should not be applied excessively. If you have thin or oily hair, you'll just need a small amount of serum; if your hair is thicker and drier, you'll need a little more.

How Do You Choose The Best Serum For Your Hair?

Hair serums are silicone-based solutions that coat and smooth your hair, reducing frizz and flyaways. Choose one that is appropriate for your hair type and concern:

1. Frizzy, dry hair

Serums with components like rosewood, castor, and marula should be used. They all supply your hair with great moisture.

2. Split Ends

For damaged hair and split ends, look for components like keratin (a hair protein) in serum.

3. Hair Damaged By Heat And Pollution

Choose lavender and jojoba oils, which are gentle on your hair, detangle it, and are very light.

4. Long Hair

If you have exceptionally thick hair, serums packed with argan and moroccan oils should be your first choice.

5. Hair with Curls

This hair type requires a little more effort to maintain. In serums, look for components like jojoba, sweet almond oil, argan, and marula. These provide an additional hydration boost.

6. Hair that has been colored or chemically treated

Look for serums that contain coconut oil and green tea extracts..

Is Hair Serum Necessary?

For all the right reasons, hair serum has become an integral element of our hair care routines. Shampoos, while effective, might cause the hair cuticles to be exposed. Because conditioner does not effectively seal your cuticle, a layer of serum is required. Serums should be a component of your hair care routine if you can't live without heat styling your hair.
