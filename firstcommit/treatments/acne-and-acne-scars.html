Acne & Acne Scars

Clear active acne and smooth the marks it leaves behind.

Non-SurgicalSessions: 3 – 6Anaesthesia: Topical numbing

Overview

About Acne & Acne Scars

Acne is treated medically first — with a prescription skincare regimen, oral medications where needed and in-clinic peels or light therapy to bring breakouts under control.

Once acne is quiet, scars are addressed with a combination approach: microneedling with PRP, fractional laser resurfacing, subcision for tethered scars, TCA CROSS for ice-pick scars, and fillers for volume loss. Most patients see steady improvement over a series of sessions.

Benefits

  • Fewer breakouts and less inflammation
  • Visibly smoother skin texture
  • Combination protocols customised to scar type
  • Minimal downtime

Ideal candidates

  • Active or recurrent acne
  • Rolling, boxcar or ice-pick scars
  • Post-acne pigmentation
  • All skin types (protocols adjusted for Indian skin)
Your Journey

What to expect

  1. Skin analysis

    Identify acne type, scar type and skin tone.

  2. Control

    Medical therapy and peels to settle active acne.

  3. Resurface

    Microneedling / laser / subcision sessions 4–6 weeks apart.

  4. Maintain

    Skincare and sun protection to hold the result.

A note from Dr. Johar: Every patient is different. The details above are a general guide — your consultation will give you a personalised plan, timeline and estimate.
