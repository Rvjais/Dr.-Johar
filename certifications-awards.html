A career built on trust

Dr. Manoj K Johar is widely regarded as one of the best plastic surgeons in Noida and Delhi-NCR. As Director & Chief Surgeon at Max Healthcare, he leads plastic, aesthetic and reconstructive surgery across three hospitals and is a regular speaker, teacher and DocTalk host on cosmetic and reconstructive surgery.

  • Member — Association of Plastic Surgeons of India (APSI)
  • Member — Indian Association of Aesthetic Plastic Surgeons (IAAPS)
  • Fellow — International College of Surgeons
  • Registered — Medical Council of India / National Medical Commission
  • Director & Chief Surgeon — Kratam Healthcare
