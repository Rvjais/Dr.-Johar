Privacy Policy

Information we collect

When you contact us through this website, by phone, WhatsApp or email, we may collect your name, contact details, and any health information you choose to share with us for the purpose of providing care and responding to your enquiry.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and schedule appointments
  • To provide medical care and follow-up
  • To send you information you have requested, such as newsletters (you can unsubscribe at any time)

Confidentiality

Your medical information is treated as confidential and handled in accordance with applicable Indian law and professional medical ethics. We do not sell your personal information.

Your rights
