Director & Chief Surgeon

Dr. Manoj K Johar

Plastic, Aesthetic & Reconstructive Surgery

Dr. Manoj K. Johar is a renowned cosmetic and plastic surgeon in Noida with extensive experience in both cosmetic and reconstructive procedures. He is dedicated to providing highly personalised care that meets the unique needs of each patient.

Whether you are seeking cosmetic surgery to enhance your appearance or reconstructive surgery to address a medical condition, his compassionate approach and commitment to excellence have made him one of the most trusted plastic surgeons in the Delhi-NCR region.

As Director & Chief Surgeon at Max Healthcare, Dr. Johar leads a multidisciplinary team across three hospitals — Noida, Vaishali and Patparganj — offering the complete spectrum of surgical and non-surgical aesthetic treatments, complex reconstruction, craniofacial surgery, limb preservation and cancer reconstruction.

Principal Consultant

Dr. Rohit Chandra

Department of Plastic Surgery

Dr. Rohit Chandra is a Principal Consultant in the Department of Plastic Surgery and a core member of Dr. Johar's Plastic Surgery Group. He works across aesthetic, reconstructive and microsurgical procedures with a focus on safe, natural-looking outcomes.

He is closely involved in patient counselling, pre-operative planning and post-operative care, ensuring every patient receives continuity of care from consultation to complete recovery.

Areas of expertise

Aesthetic surgeryReconstructive surgeryMicrosurgeryBody contouring

Dr. Kamlesh Kumar

Department of Plastic Surgery

Dr. Kamlesh Kumar is a Senior Consultant in the Department of Plastic Surgery. He brings wide experience in trauma reconstruction, burns, hand surgery and cosmetic procedures, and is known for his meticulous surgical technique.

He is an integral part of the group's reconstructive and limb-preservation programmes.

Areas of expertise

Trauma & burns reconstructionHand surgeryCosmetic surgeryLimb preservation

Dr. Lalit Agrawal

Department of Plastic Surgery

Dr. Lalit Agrawal is a Senior Consultant in the Department of Plastic Surgery with a special interest in aesthetic surgery of the face and body, and in scar and wound management.

He works closely with Dr. Johar on complex cases and leads many of the group's non-surgical and minimally invasive rejuvenation services.

Senior Director

Dr. Sanjeev Dua

Department of NeuroSciences

Dr. Sanjeev Dua is Senior Director in the Department of NeuroSciences and collaborates with Dr. Johar's group on craniofacial and cranio-synostosis surgery, skull-base reconstruction and complex head & neck cases.

This multidisciplinary partnership allows children and adults with craniofacial conditions to receive coordinated neurosurgical and plastic-surgical care under one roof.

NeurosurgeryCraniofacial collaborationSkull-base reconstruction
