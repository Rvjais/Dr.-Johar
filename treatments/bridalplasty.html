Bridalplasty

Your wedding-day glow — planned from months ahead.

Non-SurgicalDuration: 3 – 6 month planAnaesthesia: None / topical
Book a ConsultationAsk about this treatment
Overview

About Bridalplasty

Bridalplasty is a personalised programme of treatments timed before your wedding: skin brightening, medical facials, peels, laser hair removal, acne control, Botox and fillers, body contouring and hair health — scheduled so everything settles perfectly by the big day.

We build a countdown calendar starting 3–6 months out, with the gentlest treatments in the final weeks. Grooms are welcome too.

Benefits

  • Radiant, camera-ready skin
  • Structured 3–6 month timeline
  • Combines skin, hair and body treatments
  • For brides and grooms

Ideal candidates

  • Brides and grooms 3–6 months before the wedding
  • Anyone preparing for a major event
  • All skin types
  • Those wanting a safe, planned approach
Your Journey

What to expect

  1. Consult (3–6 months out)

    Skin analysis and countdown plan.

  2. Correct

    Peels, lasers, acne and pigmentation control.

  3. Refine (6–8 weeks out)

    Botox, fillers, skin boosters.

  4. Glow (1–2 weeks out)

    Medical facial and hydration.

A note from Dr. Johar: Every patient is different. The details above are a general guide — your consultation will give you a personalised plan, timeline and estimate.
